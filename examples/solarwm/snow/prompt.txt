A snow-covered village square is surrounded by timber houses with steep roofs, stone foundations, fenced paths, stacked firewood, and a small covered well. Packed snow tracks connect the buildings and descend along a narrow road toward a frozen lake bordered by dark conifers and leafless trees. Rough logs, weathered boards, stone walls, ice, and frosted glass create varied materials across the settlement. Pale overcast winter light casts gentle blue shadows, while warm window glow adds restrained amber accents. The houses, fences, well, paths, wood piles, and distant lake remain fixed within a calm, layered winter environment.
