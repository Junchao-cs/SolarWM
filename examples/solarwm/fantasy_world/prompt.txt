An ancient stone castle is built into a forested cliff above a broad river gorge, with weathered towers linked by arched bridges, terraced courtyards, mossy stairways, waterfalls beside the walls, and distant mountain peaks. Stone, timber, water, moss, and cloud textures remain sharply defined across the connected architecture. Narrow stairways pass between retaining walls and planted terraces, while the river gorge opens beneath the bridges and the waterfalls descend toward lower rocks. Soft blue evening light mixes with warm firelight in fixed windows, creating deep gray, green, blue, and amber tones. The castle remains majestic, stable, and physically coherent within the layered cliff environment.
